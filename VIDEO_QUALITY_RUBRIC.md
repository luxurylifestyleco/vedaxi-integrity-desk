# Elastic Web video quality gate

Target: 16:9, 480p final delivery. Neutral American narration. Approved human identities; Pip pink and Atlas blue. Vedaxi arrow logo, Playfair Display headings and Inter body. VDX styling is confined to phone UI.

Score each dimension 0–4: 0 broken, 1 major defects, 2 visible defects, 3 acceptable, 4 strong. Pass requires every dimension >=3 and no hard failure. Uninspected dimensions remain UNVERIFIED.

1. Identity: match approved face, hair, costume, agent silhouette and proportions across the shot.
2. Realism/anatomy: believable skin, eyes, hands, body proportions, contact and material response; agent anatomy stays coherent.
3. Motion: natural timing, restrained acting, stable objects, coherent camera and lighting; no morphing or flicker.
4. Story: one readable action per shot. Customer budget/specification/delivery constraints remain visible in the edit. Financing eligibility and final customer consent precede order execution.
5. Brand/composition: correct Vedaxi palette and hierarchy; exact arrow logo and fonts composited from approved assets; no invented logos or generated message text. Pink/blue remain agent identity accents.
6. Technical: probe dimensions, duration, frame rate and decode integrity; review beginning/middle/end frames plus motion playback; final narration intelligible with score beneath speech.

Hard failures: identity drift; malformed hands/limbs; duplicate characters; incorrect logo or message text; unauthorized purchase implication; major temporal artifacts; corrupt render.

Workflow: inspect representative first shots before extending production. Record job IDs and review evidence. Correct a failed shot and rerun its affected checks. Do not label clips as a complete film; final assembly, audio, typography and end-to-end review are separate gates.
